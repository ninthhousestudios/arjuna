A follow-up on the planet-health ranking. Going back over round 2, I think we
applied your "the sign is always stronger than the aspect" rule too
literally, so I've drafted a v3. There's also one question that only you can
answer.

**What went wrong in v2.** We ranked every planet by its sign/conjunction
score first and used aspects only to break exact ties. In practice aspects
almost never counted: across the 16 test charts they changed the order in
about 1 comparison out of 11. The clearest casualty is my own chart. My
Saturn is moolatrikona, but it's aspected by the Sun, the waning Moon, and
Mars, all enemies and all malefic. v2 still ranked it as my *healthiest*
planet.

**The v3 fix.** I read your rule as one-to-one: an exaltation outweighs a
Mars aspect, starved-in-sign outweighs starved-by-aspect. So v3 counts
aspects at half weight. A full-strength aspect is always weaker than the same
condition coming from the sign, but several aspects can add up. Under v3 my
Saturn drops from 1st to 6th of 7. Most other charts change very little, and
every chart's healthiest planet now has a genuinely positive score.

**The question: when a malefic enemy aspects a planet, should it count
twice?** Following Ernst, a planet is *agitated* when it's conjunct the Sun
or aspected by a malefic enemy, and *starved* when it's aspected by any
enemy. So every malefic-enemy aspect makes the planet both starved and
agitated, and costs about two-thirds more than a benefic enemy's aspect.
Saturn gets hit hardest, because its enemies (Sun, Moon, Mars) are all
malefic. With my Saturn, for example:

- **Count both** (what we do now): Saturn is 6th of 7.
- **Count only the worse one** (starved): Saturn is 3rd of 7.

Or something in between: both count, but the agitation counts for less when
the aspect is already starving the planet.
